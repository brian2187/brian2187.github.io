Idaho Joint Revocable Living Trust Template

Fill-in-the-blank starting point for a joint revocable living trust in Idaho (Sandpoint / Sagle area).

IMPORTANT LEGAL DISCLAIMER: This is a template only, not legal advice. Idaho is a community property state. Have any completed document reviewed and customized by a licensed Idaho estate planning attorney before signing or funding. An unfunded trust does not avoid probate. Coordinate with powers of attorney and healthcare directives.

What the Template Includes

  • Joint Revocable Living Trust for husband and wife
  • Community property language intended to satisfy Idaho Code § 32-906A
  • Both spouses as initial Co-Trustees (either may act alone)
  • Successor Trustee blanks with incapacity provisions
  • Distribution language: surviving spouse first, then primary beneficiaries after both have died
  • Schedule A for listing assets (real property, accounts, vehicles, personal property)
  • Notary acknowledgment for Bonner County / State of Idaho
  • Built-in step-by-step instructions

Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Gather information
Full legal names of both spouses, current address, list of major assets (especially the house with legal description from the current deed), desired successor trustee(s) with contact info, and beneficiaries (usually children) with how shares should be divided after both spouses have died.
Step 2: Fill in every blank
Complete all [bracketed] fields in the Word document carefully. Decide specific gifts (if any) and residual distribution.
Step 3: Have an attorney review it (strongly recommended)
Especially important for real estate and community property. Local Sandpoint options with free consultations:
• Sandpoint Estate Planning: (208) 268-1203
• Pinnacle Estate Planning (Jason Gray): (208) 449-1213
A review-only session is usually much less expensive than a full plan.
Step 4: Sign before a notary
Both spouses sign in front of a notary public in Idaho.
Step 5: Fund the Trust (the most important step)
Retitle assets into the Trust name (example: “John and Jane Doe, Trustees of the Doe Joint Revocable Living Trust dated [date]”). For the house, prepare and record a new deed. Unfunded assets still go through probate.
Step 6: Complete the rest of the plan
Also prepare Pour-over Wills, Durable Powers of Attorney (financial), and Advance Healthcare Directives for each spouse.
Step 7: Store and share
Keep the original in a safe place. Give copies to the Successor Trustee(s). Review every few years or after major life changes.

Cost Context

  • Online services (LegalZoom, Trust & Will, Nolo): typically $150–$650 for Idaho-specific documents
  • Attorney-drafted joint plans in Idaho: commonly $2,000–$3,500 for straightforward estates
  • Hybrid approach (this template or online + local attorney review + deed): often the best balance of cost and safety

Not a substitute for professional legal counsel. Funding and attorney review are essential.
